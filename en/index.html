IBAN Generator

Structurally valid IBANs (mod 97 check digits) for the 27 countries of the European Union. No ads, no account, no payment.

Quick:

    How it works

    Pick a country (the one detected from your browser is preselected), enter a quantity, then click Generate. Each IBAN is built from the national format of that country (length, bank code, branch code, account number), then the check digits are computed: the national one where the country uses one, and the international IBAN check digits (ISO 7064, mod 97-10). The result passes IBAN validators.

    The bank codes used are real ones: BNP Paribas, ING, Santander, Rabobank, UniCredit, Deutsche Bank. That is what makes the IBAN look credible. The account number, however, is random: no generated IBAN matches an existing account.

    Frequently asked questions

    Are the generated IBANs valid?

    Yes, structurally. Each IBAN follows the national format of the chosen country (ISO 13616), the international check digits (ISO 7064, mod 97-10) and, for Belgium, Spain, France, Italy, Portugal, Finland, Slovenia and Estonia, the national check digits.

    Do they match real bank accounts?

    No. The bank code is real so that the format looks realistic, but the account number is random. No transfer can ever reach an IBAN generated here.

    What is an IBAN generator for?

    For testing: payment forms, validation rules, SEPA file imports, demo or staging datasets. Using a generated IBAN to deceive someone is fraud, and it is illegal.

    Is my data sent anywhere?

    No. Everything is computed in your browser, in JavaScript. No server, no tracker, no ads, no signup.

    How many IBANs can I generate at once?

    Up to 1000 per batch, exportable as TXT or CSV with the country and the bank.

    Warning: the generated IBANs follow the national format and the check digits, but match no real bank account. Any fraudulent use is illegal.
